Zak McKracken: Between Time and Space is a point-and-click adventure game by German studio Artificial Hair Bros., originally released in 2008. It is an unofficial fan sequel of the 1988 adventure game Zak McKracken and the Alien Mindbenders and was developed without the involvement or license of LucasArts.

As in the original game, the player assumes the role of Zak McKracken, a frustrated boulevard reporter from San Francisco.
