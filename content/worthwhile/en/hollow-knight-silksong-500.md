Hornet ascends the kingdom of Pharloom and uses faster acrobatics, tools, and silk. It continues from Hollow Knight, but with a new height of movement, tool crafting, and Hornet's mobility it rebuilds the rhythm of exploration and of combat; Team Cherry confirmed a launch of September 4, 2025.

Hornet in Hollow Knight: Silksong ascends the kingdom of Pharloom, and the player immediately feels a different body from the Knight's: faster acrobatics, tools that are prepared, and silk that feeds both combat and movement. The map is still a metroidvania joined by shortcuts, but the rhythm is no longer the one you brought from Hallownest. Combat wants more air and more preparation of a tool than a patient retreat into one strike. The loss of the old habit is part of the opening hour, not a control mistake.

![In the game](/img/worthwhile-games/media/500-screenshot-1.jpg)

Team Cherry, Ari Gibson and William Pellen, released the game on September 4, 2025 on computer, on Switch including Switch 2, on PlayStation, and on Xbox. The small Australian studio, after years of silence, continues from Hollow Knight in 2017 and from an announcement that had hung in public since 2019. The decision to rebuild movement instead of merely adding areas means the sequel cannot be a bigger Hallownest in a new coat. The consequence is that mastery of the first game does not transfer one to one, and fans learn again, which is a risk after so long a wait.

Silk and tools are an economy of the body. This is not only the heroine's cosmetics; healing, attack, and traversal draw on a shared resource and force you to choose what you spend it on in a given room. The drawing stays handmade and the world animal. The studio does not change the visual promise; it changes the timing, so the player recognizes the authors and at the same time runs into a foreign rhythm.

![Cover](/img/worthwhile-games/media/500-cover-2.jpg)

In 2025 the release is itself an event, because the waiting became part of the game's history. Beside the debuts that take the big prizes the same year, Silksong is a sequel whose burden is expectation, not the need to explain who the studio is. Pharloom is an ascent, not a descent into an ever quieter kingdom. The direction of the map is thematic: Hornet is climbing somewhere, and the game adjusts the vertical to that.

It is early to claim which chamber will age. Already one can separate what holds, the acrobatics and the feeling that a tool has more than one use, from what will be the period tax of a long development, that is the comparison with the idea the community spent eight years drawing for itself. The courage of a small team to change the hero and the cadence holds. It is still the best current example that a metroidvania sequel can be a new grammar of movement, not only a new set of keys.

Difficulty is part of the tone, as it already was at the studio. Anyone who expected a gentler Hornet as a reward for waiting expected a different contract from the one Team Cherry writes. In class, set it immediately after Hollow Knight and let students describe the first hour in verbs only. The difference in the verbs is a whole lecture on why a sequel need not protect the old hand.
