The artist Andreas Maler investigates crimes in a small Bavarian town during the Reformation. Typography, handwriting, and historical research are part of both the UI and the theme; no universally confirmed "correct" detective answer is guaranteed.

Andreas Maler in Pentiment investigates a murder in a Bavarian town at the start of the Reformation, and the player spends the day not managing to speak to everyone. A meal with a family, a visit to the abbey, and an accusation have opening hours, because time is shared. A character's typeface changes according to who they are: a typesetter, a monk, a peasant. The interface is a historical argument about who can write and whose hand is therefore allowed to remain in the records.

Obsidian and director Josh Sawyer, known from Fallout: New Vegas, released the game through Xbox Game Studios in November 2022 as a smaller, more personal project beside the studio's big RPGs. It is not a realistic 3D world; it is an illustrated book you walk through. The decision not to give one confirmed correct answer to the crime means an accusation is an act that later years survive even when you were wrong. The consequence is a detective story about historiography, not about closing a case.

Meals and work are the budget of the day. The studio forces you to choose whose life you will get to know, and thereby turns a limitation into historical credibility: a stranger will not manage everything. Latin, print, and disputes about faith are not a quiz on the margin. They are the reasons the village holds together or tears.

In 2022 this is a surprise from a big RPG studio: short, textual, with no loot ladder. Beside Elden Ring the same year it shows that Obsidian can still make a game that stands on a sentence. The acts skip years. Your old accusation returns as a social fact, not as a flag in a menu that could be switched off.

Little has aged, because the game is short and is not operated as a service. Some minigames of copying text are a period ornament and are not the reason to sit at the table. The typography and the uncertainty hold. It is still the best example of how to turn type into characterization and a detective story into an admission that the past cannot be closed with a stamp.

Andreas as a painter sees the world through a commission and through his own education. The player is not a universal hero; he is a specific craftsman with limited access to the truth. In class, use it for who writes history. Ask which character students would accuse a second time if they were allowed to read only that character's type and that character's table.

Pentiment is a 2022 adventure role-playing video game developed by Obsidian Entertainment and published by Xbox Game Studios. Pentiment is a narrative adventure role-playing video game played from a 2D perspective.

He first pitched what would become Pentiment to Feargus Urquhart in the 1990s, when the two were working at Black Isle Studios. In 2025, Pentiment was one of several games that were affected by a security vulnerability in the Unity engine; it was removed from digital storefronts from October 3 to October 7, 2025 to address the issue.

Pentiment received "generally favorable" reviews from critics, according to review aggregator website Metacritic.
