Animal Crossing: New Horizons is a 2020 social simulation game developed and published by Nintendo for the Nintendo Switch. It is the fifth main installment in the Animal Crossing series, following Animal Crossing: New Leaf.

In New Horizons, the player controls a character who moves to a deserted island after purchasing a getaway package from Tom Nook, accomplishes assigned tasks, and develops the island as they choose. Development of New Horizons began in 2012, following the release of Animal Crossing: New Leaf.

New Horizons received acclaim from critics, who praised its gameplay and customization options, although its subsequent updates were given mixed response. It has since been considered one of the greatest games of all time, and is the best-selling game in the Animal Crossing series, the second best-selling game on the Nintendo Switch, the best-selling game of all time in Japan, and the 12th best-selling video game in history.

As in previous Animal Crossing games, New Horizons is a life simulation game that is played in real time. Weather also adjusts to the seasons of the Northern or Southern Hemisphere, depending on the player's real-world location, a first for the Animal Crossing series.

The other currency "Bells", a staple in the Animal Crossing franchise, can be used to purchase other goods and services. Animal Crossing: New Horizons supports both local and online co-op gameplay, with up to four players locally and eight players online able to occupy an island at any given time, an option available through the Dodo Airlines.

Since the game depends on seasonal changes, New Horizons received frequent updates, adding new seasonal items, events, and sometimes new gameplay features. Each respective update also featured popular characters from past Animal Crossing games.

In an October 2021 New Horizons-focused Nintendo Direct, Nintendo announced the version 2.0 update would be released the following month. Announced alongside the 2.0 update was paid downloadable content (DLC), entitled Animal Crossing: New Horizons – Happy Home Paradise.
