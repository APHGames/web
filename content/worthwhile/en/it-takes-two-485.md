Divorcing parents are turned into dolls and must cooperate. Mandatory two-player cooperation constantly changes asymmetric abilities; every system is designed around communication between two people.

In It Takes Two divorcing parents wake as dolls, and the game will not let you continue at all without a second person. In every chapter the player receives different asymmetrical abilities and has to say them aloud, because you cannot see the whole of your partner's on-screen instructions. Cooperation is mandatory even in puzzles that look like jumping. One holds, the other hits, and the studio deliberately offers a loner no substitute mode.

Hazelight and Josef Fares, who had already shown two bodies in Brothers, released the game through EA Originals in March 2021. Friend's Pass means the second player does not have to buy a copy. In 2021 it takes The Game Awards' game of the year. Unreal Engine carries a constant exchange of genre wrappers: magnets, time, a garden, snow. The technology serves the possibility that a chapter can be thrown away once it exhausts an idea, and the next can begin with different controls.

The decision to forbid single player means every mechanic is designed as a sentence between two people. The consequence is a game that cannot be streamed the same way it is played, because half the information is in the other room or on the other couch. The story of a marriage is broad comedy with a serious skeleton. Fares bets that the transformation of abilities will hold attention even where the dialogue is deliberately simple.

In 2021 this is an argument against live services: a short, expensive, cooperative campaign with no season. Beside A Way Out it is more generous in how many games fit inside one. Split screen and online are equal paths. The studio counts on couples sitting side by side and on those sitting in another city.

Some of the jokes about the relationship have aged, as have chapters whose idea is thinner than the opening transformation. The demand for two people does not fall behind. The pace of exchanging verbs holds. It is still a textbook in how to design asymmetry so that silence in a pair is a loss.

Every boss and every puzzle counts on one player seeing something the other does not have. Inequality of information is the material, not a camera problem. In class, let two students play it and forbid the others to advise. You will see how much of the design is in what has to be said, not in what is on the screen.

It Takes Two is a 2021 cooperative action-adventure game developed by Hazelight Studios and published by Electronic Arts. It Takes Two received critical acclaim, with reviewers praising its cooperative design, mechanical variety, creativity, and connection between gameplay and story.

To progress in the game, the two players must fulfill different roles and achieve their objectives cooperatively.]] It Takes Two is a cooperative action-adventure game with elements from platform games. Fares said that Hazelight used a guiding word for each of its games: Brothers was about "loss", A Way Out was about "trust", and It Takes Two was about "collaboration".

It Takes Two was published under the EA Originals label, allowing Hazelight to retain full creative control while receiving most of the game's profit after development costs were recouped. Before the game's release, Take-Two Interactive filed a trademark claim against It Takes Two.

It Takes Two received "generally favorable" reviews from critics, according to review aggregator Metacritic.
