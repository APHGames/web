Antigravity machines race at extreme speed on spatial tracks. 60fps speed, precise physics, and hard difficulty are the peak of pure futuristic racing.

In F-Zero GX you drive an antigravity machine so fast that the track is not a circuit but a solid: loops, cylinders, and vertical walls you are still stuck to. Throttle, grip, and boost are three decisions in every meter. A mistake in lean does not drop you a few places; it throws you off the track.

![Cover](/img/worthwhile-games/media/324-cover-1.jpg)

Amusement Vision, the team around Toshihiro Nagoshi from Sega, made it in 2003 for Nintendo on the GameCube. A collaboration of Sega and Nintendo is itself an industrial peculiarity of that year. The game aims at 60 frames a second, because at this speed smoothness is a condition of readability, not a luxury.

The consequence of the hard physics is a high ceiling. You can know the track and still lose it on decisions that last a fraction of a second. In 2003, beside Project Gotham and Mario Kart, this is a race stripped of the city, of licensed cars, and of forgiveness. Pure futuristic driving has its peak here.

The difficulty has aged only in the sense that it still stops many players. The feeling of speed and control itself has not, and other futuristic racers are still measured by it. The custom-machine mode and the story cups are extra content. The core is a time trial against a machine that does not forgive late braking.

The arcade sibling AX shows that the game also had the cabinet in mind. The home version nevertheless remained the one history remembers. The series then went quiet for many years. GX is thus not an introduction but a peak after which the genre had nowhere to continue in the same sentence.

For students of driving physics it is a lesson that readability at extreme speed is solved by frame rate and by the shape of the track, not by an arrow on the asphalt. When you switch it on today, take the first laps as a lesson in falling. The game first teaches you what a mistake looks like, and only then lets you into a clean lap.
