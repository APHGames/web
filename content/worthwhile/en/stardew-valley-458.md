The player restores an inherited farm and relationships in Pelican Town. A single author synthesized Harvest Moon, crafting, relationships, and a light RPG; the title strengthened the modern "cozy" game tradition.

In Stardew Valley you inherit an overgrown farm, and in the morning the player waters, in the afternoon they mine, and in the evening they either manage, or fail, to have a conversation with someone in town before the saloon closes. The community center wants bundles of gifts from the whole landscape. JojaMart offers a monetary shortcut and thereby turns the economy into an ethical choice, not only an optimization.

![Cover](/img/worthwhile-games/media/458-cover-1.jpg)

Eric Barone under the name ConcernedApe wrote the game alone for several years in C# and in February 2016 released it with the publisher Chucklefish. It is a deliberate answer to Harvest Moon: a farm, relationships, seasons, and a light RPG in one person. XNA and the later ports show the craft of a solo author who is not waiting for an engine department. Updates over the years, including co-op and new areas, turn the game into a service that one person still holds.

The decision to tie the day's energy to the clock means missing a visit is design, not an interface mistake. The consequence is a calm pressure: the farm grows, but the festival calendar will not wait. Fishing, the mines, and relationships are three different minigames on the same clock. Anyone who wants only one still runs into the fact that the town needs the others too.

In 2016 the game strengthens the wave of so-called cozy games. It is not the first, but it is the one a large part of players take as the contemporary shape of the genre, because it is available on computer and still growing. Pelican Town is small and repeated on purpose. Familiarity comes from knowing who stands by the river on Tuesday, not from the size of the map.

Some of the relationship dialogues have aged, as has the early interface, which assumes patience. The amount of later content can also hide how clean the first year was. The daily loop holds, and so does the feeling that work in the field is a rest from other games. That is historically more important than any individual festival.

The seasons change what you are even allowed to plant. The design of the time of year is a harder rule than many combat systems, and yet it feels gentle. In class, take it as a lesson in authorial endurance: one person can synthesize a genre if they last for years at the same farm, in the game and outside it.

Stardew Valley sold roughly 425,000 copies across Steam and GOG.com in its first two weeks, and more than a million within two months. Valve reported that Stardew Valley was in the top 24 revenue-generating games on Steam during 2016.

By the end of 2017, Stardew Valley had sold more than 3.5 million copies across all platforms. By February 2026, Stardew Valley had sold over 50 million copies, 26 million of which were on PC and 7.9 million on Nintendo Switch.

Stardew Valley received three nominations at the 2016 Golden Joystick Awards, winning the Breakthrough Award. Several Publications placed Stardew Valley on their year-end lists, including The Guardian (5th), Paste (8th), Polygon (8th), Slant Magazine (25th) and The Verge.
