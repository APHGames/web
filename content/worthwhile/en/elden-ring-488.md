The Tarnished explores the Lands Between and fights for the Elden Ring. Souls combat was carried into an open landscape, where orientation is supported by vertical geography and discovery instead of a catalog of icons. PC Gamer ranks it among the highest contemporary PC canon.

The Lands Between in Elden Ring are open, and on the horse Torrent the player rides around what they do not yet dare, collects map fragments, and descends into legacy dungeons that are still dark Souls levels in the middle of a landscape. Stamina combat, spirit ashes, and weapon builds remain the language of FromSoftware. Grace on the map guides you, but it is not a catalog of icons; a hill and a tower still do the main navigation.

![Cover](/img/worthwhile-games/media/488-cover-1.jpg)

Hidetaka Miyazaki directed the game and George R. R. Martin wrote the mythological background before the production itself. Bandai Namco released it in February 2022. In 2022 it takes The Game Awards' game of the year, and the Shadow of the Erdtree expansion arrives in 2024. The decision to carry Souls combat into a landscape without a mandatory order means a boss you are stuck on can be ridden around. The consequence is a freedom pure Dark Souls did not have, and also repeated catacombs that thin the landscape in places.

Spirit ashes let you summon help without a second player. The studio thereby changes the contract of solitude, and some fans take it as an opening of the game, some as a softening. Field bosses at night and hand-built ramparts are not the same thing. The game teaches you to recognize when you are in an open-world system and when you are in a level that would stand even if it were closed.

![In the game](/img/worthwhile-games/media/488-screenshot-2.jpg)

In 2022 it becomes the center of the argument about whether an open world still needs markers. The PC version suffers from smoothness at launch, which does not escape notice in a game this watched. Martin's background is a backdrop, not the script of every quest. The player still assembles the lore from items and from silence, as is the studio's habit, only more of it on horseback.

The tuning of later bosses has aged, and they can overwhelm you with one combo, as have those repeated smaller catacombs. The expectation that every next game will be Elden Ring is a debt it made for the others. The legacy dungeons, the builds, and the feeling that you found a place the map could not yet name all hold. That is why the game stays in the canon even outside the Souls community.

Multiplayer marks and invasions remain the weather of the world. You are not entirely alone, even though you ride most of the way without a word. In class, compare one path along the milestones of grace with one path students ride because they see a castle. The second is the one the map was drawn for.
