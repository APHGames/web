Poker combinations are modified by Jokers, multipliers, and ever more absurd synergies. From a simple, familiar set of poker hands it creates exponential buildcrafting; the GDCA 2025 awarded it Game of the Year, Best Debut, the Innovation Award, and Best Design.

In Balatro you play poker hands and then break them with jokers that multiply the score, change suits, or punish your own greed. Between blinds the player goes to the shop, deletes cards from the deck, and watches a familiar straight turn into an absurd number. The ante, the small blind, and the boss blind are the structure of a run. A boss often forbids exactly what your build stood on, so flexibility is part of power, not only a large multiplier.

LocalThunk made the game alone, and Playstack released it in February 2024. The decision to take rules half the players have already seen at a table means a short tutorial and deep combinatorics. The consequence is that a newcomer understands a pair and an expert solves an order of multiplication that never existed on paper poker.

Jokers are relics from a deckbuilder carried onto five cards. The studio thereby joins Slay the Spire with a game it does not have to explain from zero. The shop economy forces you to destroy the deck as well as improve it. A surplus card is a slower cycle, which is the same lesson as in other deckbuilders, only in the language of suits and ranks.

In 2024 this is the surprise of the year: a small game that takes over the design argument beside big sequels. It sits in a pocket of time, on computer and on consoles, and it is still one screen of a table. The betting is not a casino simulation with a payout table. It is a pretext for an exponential chain that is supposed to break on a boss's limit.

Only the speed with which it became a pattern for imitations has aged. The table itself is still readable, and the solo authorship is still part of why it feels so concentrated. The conversion of a known rule into systemic depth holds. It is still the best contemporary example that innovation need not begin with a new verb, but with a new multiplication of an old one.

A loss on a boss blind is usually intelligible: a suit was missing, a multiplier was missing, or you bet everything on one hand. The game does not lie about why the number was not enough. Balatro is a 2024 poker-themed roguelike deck-building game developed by solo developer LocalThunk and published by Playstack. Inspired by the card game big two and the roguelike video game Luck Be a Landlord, LocalThunk developed Balatro over two and a half years.

Another popular mod for Balatro is the Multiplayer mod, which allows players to face off against another player during a Nemesis Blind, in which whoever scores the highest is able to take a life from their opponent. Balatro was developed by LocalThunk, an anonymous solo developer based in Saskatchewan, Canada.

Shortly after the game's release, Balatro s PEGI rating was changed from 3+ to 18+ for "prominent gambling imagery" due to PEGI's strict rules on portraying gambling, causing the game to be temporarily removed from sale in certain territories. The rating reprisal was successfully appealed in February 2025, and along with Luck be a Landlord, Balatro was reclassified with a 12+ rating, recognizing that the fantasy elements of the game distinguished it from gambling.

After YouTube changed its policies in March 2025, many Balatro videos were marked as 18+ for "depictions or promotions of online casino sites or apps," blocking these videos from child YouTube accounts. The ports released on September 26, 2024, including a Balatro+ version for Apple Arcade.

Various pieces of official merchandise have also been released; A physical deck of playing cards using Balatro pixel art graphics and a 2026 calendar (featuring photographs of Ben Starr cosplaying various jokers) were released by Fangamer, various t-shirts with mascot Jimbo and various other cards are sold by Playstack. LocalThunk had planned to release a major update to Balatro during 2025, but by September 2025, opted to push that to 2026, stating his desire to continue to keep the game's development as his hobby and avoid crunch.

In a February 2026 post commemorating Balatro 's second anniversary, he confirmed the update is still being worked on. Balatro-themed content has been added to numerous other games through official collaborations, including: Reception.

Abbie Stone from PC Gamer described gameplay as "strategizing your way to success," Charlie Brooker, the creator of Black Mirror, called it "possibly the most addictive thing ever created" and that once it was released for mobile devices, "humankind's activity is going to drop about 25%"; Balatro was subsequently briefly shown in the "Hotel Reverie" episode of Black Mirror seventh season, released in April 2025. Film director James Gunn called Balatro a "great game", and spent much of the downtime during production of Superman playing the game.

With the success of Balatro, LocalThunk kept to a low profile, not wanting much attention on his real-world identity and avoiding the stress that would come with it. While LocalThunk and his partner had been present at the Game Awards, he told no one else who they were, and opted to allow representatives from PlayStack to accept the awards Balatro won on his behalf.

A similar arrangement was made for the Game Developers Choice Awards, with PlayStack representatives accepting the awards for Balatro while LocalThunk remained anonymous at the event. Following its popularity, the game has inspired significant activity from the player community, including memes, the integration of other games within it, and an unofficial port to the Apple Watch developed in January 2025, called Wee Balatro, which is awaiting the author's approval.
