Tomb Raider, also known under Lara Croft: Tomb Raider from 1996 to 2008, is a media franchise that originated with an action-adventure video game series created by British developer Core Design. Development of the first Tomb Raider video game began in 1994; it was released two years later.

The sixth game, Tomb Raider: The Angel of Darkness, faced difficulties during development and was considered a failure at release. The Tomb Raider series had sold over 100 million units worldwide by 2024, while the entire franchise generated close to $1.2 billion in revenue by 2002.
