The side view, the bullet hell, and the more open map are not minigames for relief; they are ways of seeing the same war for a while through different controls.

The decision to hide a substantial part of the story behind repeated completion means anyone who leaves after the first credits played a different, smaller game. The consequence is a cult around the endings, and also a fair complaint that the opening does not say this aloud. The philosophy of names, bodies, and meaning is not wallpaper. The endings argue about whether an android and a machine are even allowed to want their own reason to exist.

Nier: Automata is a 2017 action role-playing game developed by PlatinumGames and published by Square Enix. Nier: Automata was originally released for the PlayStation 4 and Windows via Steam.

Nier: Automata is set during a proxy war between alien-created Machines and human-crafted androids, focusing on the actions of combat android 2B, scanner android 9S, and rogue prototype A2. Production began in 2014, with series creator Yoko Taro, producer Yosuke Saito, and lead composer Keiichi Okabe reprising their roles in Nier.

The goal was to make a sequel that would be faithful to Nier while improving the combat system. Nier: Automata was announced at E3 2015; it received stage plays and novels expanding its narrative, and both downloadable content (DLC) and crossovers with other games.

Sales surpassed expectations; , Nier: Automata had sold over copies worldwide. After the release of Nier, director Yoko Taro and Square Enix producer Yosuke Saito wanted to create a sequel.

Saito spoke to assistant producer Yuki Yokoyama, who was unwilling due to low sales of Nier.
