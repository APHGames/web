Pitfall Harry overcomes pits, crocodiles, and vines in a connected jungle. A long connected world showed that a cartridge could offer a more extensive adventure than a single scoring screen.

Pitfall Harry runs through the jungle, jumps pits, climbs vines, and avoids crocodiles and scorpions while collecting treasures. The world is connected sideways. It is not one screen with a score, but a series of places you walk between, and the time on the cartridge is limited.
