An astronaut explores a solar system during a repeating 22-minute cycle. The only lasting "upgrade" is the player's knowledge; the whole progression thus exists in human memory, not in an inventory.

In Outer Wilds you wake at a campfire, board a model solar system, and after twenty-two minutes a supernova burns you. The player carries away from death only what they understood: an inscription, a physics trick, or the courage to fly into the dark bramble. The ship, the suit, and the signalscope are the entire equipment. There is no damage upgrade; there is a log that holds hypotheses for you, because human memory is the real inventory.

![Artwork](/img/worthwhile-games/media/475-artwork-1.jpg)

Mobius Digital and Alex Beachum released the game, grown from a student project at USC, through Annapurna in 2019. The planets are hand-built clocks, not a random galaxy, and Andrew Prahlow wrote the music. The decision that the only persistent progression is knowledge means death returns the world to the same state and changes only the player. The consequence is exploration you cannot buy with a better suit, and that is hard to show in a video without a spoiler.

A quantum rule, a black hole, and the hourglasses of the planets are lessons that keep applying. Once you name them, the map shrinks even though the geometry stayed. The Nomai and their texts are archaeology, not a quest log with a checkbox. You are reading a civilization that was solving the same problem a little earlier and did not finish it for you.

![In the game](/img/worthwhile-games/media/475-screenshot-2.jpg)

In 2019 this is a peak of loop design. Beside roguelikes that upgrade weapons between deaths, it feels like a stricter and gentler contract. Flight is clumsy at first on purpose. The studio wants the body in weightlessness to be part of the discovery, not a comfortable camera.

Only that first awkwardness has aged for players who expect a ship from a shooter. The clocks of the system do not fall behind, because they are authored, not fashionable. Memory as the only equipment holds. Echoes of the Eye will later add another place, but the contract of twenty-two minutes is born in the base game.

![Cover](/img/worthwhile-games/media/475-cover-3.jpg)

The other travelers at their instruments are a small social map. You are not alone in space, even though most of the time you fly alone and death is your private one. In class, let students play it without advice and ask which piece of information they carried between loops. That is exactly the item the game refuses to store in a slot.

Outer Wilds is a 2019 action-adventure game developed by Mobius Digital and published by Annapurna Interactive. Outer Wilds was released for Windows, Xbox One, and PlayStation 4 in 2019, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S in 2022, and for Nintendo Switch in 2023.

Outer Wilds was positively received upon release, with most critics acclaiming its design and some criticizing the uneven difficulty of gameplay and pursuing the game's mysteries. Outer Wilds was featured in several game of the year lists for 2019 as well as game of the decade and game of the era lists, won in multiple categories at award shows, including the Best Game award at the 16th British Academy Games Awards, and is regarded as one of the greatest video games ever made.

Outer Wilds is an action-adventure game set in a small planetary system in which the player character, an unnamed space explorer referred to as "hatchling", explores and investigates its mysteries in a self-directed manner.
