Armless Rayman saves a colorful world from Mr. Dark. Extremely detailed 2D animation showed that the arrival of polygons did not mean the end of the visual relevance of sprite art.

Rayman, whose limbs are not attached in one piece, runs, swims, and throws a punch across the screen to free the beings that hold the world together. A level is a classic platformer: pits, enemies, secret passages. What is unusual is how many frames of animation Ubisoft was willing to draw for it.

![Cover](/img/worthwhile-games/media/183-cover-1.jpg)

Michel Ancel designed the game in 1995 at Ubisoft, and the first version came out on the Atari Jaguar. It received a wider life only on the PlayStation, the Saturn, and the PC, but the origin is on that less fortunate console. The engine is two-dimensional in a year when the press talks mainly about polygons. Rayman is an argument that the sprite does not end merely because 3D has begun.

The decision to invest in animation instead of a new space has a consequence. The character feels alive even without a camera behind his back, and the platformer keeps a legible jump. In 1995, beside the jumping 3D games, this is a European counterpole. Ancel is not solving how to turn a camera; he is solving what a run should look like when a hand flies separately from the body.

The difficulty and the passwords in some versions are of their period. The game can be hard in a way the beauty of the image does not excuse when you fall the third time in the same place. What has aged is precisely that hardness and the pace of some stretches. What has not aged is the crafted animation, which still looks expensive even in a shot without context.

Limbs separated from the body are not just a joke in the character design. They are the reason a punch can travel farther than the body, so the silhouette is also a rule of reach. With this, Ubisoft opens a mascot at a time when the French studio is not yet a global factory. The authorial origin is in Ancel's team, not in a brand department.

Compare the image with Donkey Kong Country. Rare pre-rendered 3D and inserted it into 2D; Ancel draws 2D richly enough that it acts as a rival to the render. In a seminar, pause a run on three frames. If the students recognize the weight of a step, they understand why the polygon did not win automatically in 1995.

It follows its protagonist character Rayman, a magical limbless creature, being renowned for his courage and determination who, with the help of his friends, must save his world from various villains. The series began with the release of the first Rayman in 1995 and has since produced multiple games across multiple systems, as well as several spin-off titles in other, non-platforming genres.

Rayman was named the Best New Character award of 1995 by Electronic Gaming Monthly. Since his debut in 1995 on the Atari Jaguar, Rayman has become a well received and recognizable video game character by players and is known for his lack of limbs.

Rayman has been the subject of a short-lived animated television series in 1999, Rayman: The Animated Series, which was produced as a tie-in to the video games, though significantly different from the source material. Rayman appears as a recurring character in the 2023 adult animated series Captain Laserhawk: A Blood Dragon Remix, voiced by David Menkin.
