A demon slayer penetrates cursed Boletaria and repeatedly risks the souls collected. Bloodstains, player messages, and invasions created an unusual asynchronously linked single-player and multiplayer, and the foundation of soulslike design.

In Demon's Souls you die in Boletaria and the souls stay at the place of death. If you die again on the way to them, they are gone, and therefore every sortie is a bet with what you have already collected. Shortcuts, the fog at the start of an area, and the Nexus hub teach the map as a memory, not as a corridor with an automatic save before a boss. The boss is a door you have to earn by already knowing the road.

![Cover](/img/worthwhile-games/media/398-cover-1.jpg)

Hidetaka Miyazaki directed it for the first time at FromSoftware in 2009, on a project that did not have a clear shape before he entered it. Sony published it in Japan; in North America Atlus brought it to the PlayStation 3, because Sony there did not take it as a first-party title. The consequence of bloodstains and of other players' messages is that the single-player game is populated by strangers' deaths. Invasions and cooperation are not a persistent world; they are visits, and yet they break the solitude without cancelling the punishment.

World tendency, a hidden mood of the world, changes the difficulty and the events according to how you and other players are doing. The system is little explained on purpose, and that is exactly why it is still discussed as a radical choice, not as a comfortable one. In 2009, beside Uncharted 2 and Arkham Asylum, it is an inhospitable Japanese action game on the PS3. It does not explode as an immediate mass template. Dark Souls two years later is what names that template for a wider audience.

Some worlds, especially the muddy test of endurance, have aged into fatigue. Death as a teacher and the layer of strangers' messages have not, and they define what is later called souls. For difficulty students it is a class about information. The game does not tell you why you died, and a message on the ground sometimes says it better than a tutorial the author would have written into a menu.

The Maiden in Black and the Monumental hold few words. The lore is in an item and in the environment, and a player who waits for an explanation does not get one. That is a contract, not an unfinished edge. The Bluepoint remake is a different object, smoother and lit differently. The original release is still the one that in 2009 risked the player leaving, and some players stayed exactly because of that.

A whole school runs on from Demon's Souls, including games that take only the punishment and not the architecture of shortcuts. In a seminar one has to separate death as a currency from death as a decoration of hard action. When you play it today, leave the first souls on the ground and decide whether you go back for them. That decision is the whole game in one minute.

Shortly after its North American release, ScrewAttack named Demon's Souls as the eighth best PS3 exclusive to date. During its first week on sale, Demon's Souls debuted at second place in the charts with over 39,000 units sold, coming in behind the previous week's top-seller Tales of the World: Radiant Mythology 2.

RPGamer awarded Demon's Souls RPG of the Year 2009, including Best Graphics and Best PS3 RPG. At the 13th Annual Interactive Achievement Awards, Demon's Souls was nominated for "Role-Playing/Massively Multiplayer Game of the Year" and "Outstanding Innovation in Gaming".

Demon's Souls has been cited as one of the greatest games of all time. Its success sparked a number of spiritual successors with similar gameplay by FromSoftware including Dark Souls, Bloodborne, and Elden Ring, which are also considered among the greatest games of all time.

Demon's Souls released as a launch title for the console on November 12, 2020.
