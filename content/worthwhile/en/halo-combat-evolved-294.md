Master Chief defends humanity on the ring world Halo. Two weapons, a regenerating shield, vehicles, and dual-stick controls stabilized the modern console FPS.

Master Chief carries only two weapons, a grenade, and a shield that returns after a moment in cover. Health itself does not return; a medkit does that, and a vehicle is not a bonus arena, it is a piece of the campaign through which you drive the war. Two sticks split movement and looking. After a decade of console FPS games that aimed through buttons, this is a contract that will become obvious, and in 2001 it is not obvious yet.

![Cover](/img/worthwhile-games/media/294-cover-1.jpg)

Bungie released it in November 2001 as an Xbox launch title. Microsoft bought the studio in 2000, and a game that began as a project for another platform becomes the reason for a new console; Martin O'Donnell holds the music, and Jason Jones stands at the founding of Bungie. The decision to limit the number of weapons and to give the shield regeneration had a consequence for rhythm. You scavenge what the fight drops, you play in cycles of cover and push, and the controller does not have to carry a whole arsenal on keys the console does not have.

In 2001 another definition of the open world and another definition of story come out beside it. Halo defines console war: allies, a vehicle, a silent hero, and a ring that is supposed to be revealed, not walked like a corridor. A number of players feel the Library stretch and part of the Flood as a narrowing after the Covenant sandbox. It is a real crack in the campaign, and it should not be swept under the ringworld in class.

What has aged is some of the repetition and the graphics of 2001. What has not aged is the rhythm of the shield, the co-op, and the feeling that the warthog is a tool of the story, not a reward in a garage. In seminar, take the second weapon and the infinite inventory away from students if they expect it in discussion. They will see how little is enough for a fight to have a decision, and how much that decision depends on what the enemy dropped.

Against GoldenEye there is a second stick here and fewer lists of objectives. Bungie takes the lesson of console shooting and exchanges it for a science fiction that does not need a film license. The ring is a revelation of space. The game hides the scale and then shows it, and a level becomes a place, which is direction, not only a skybox.

A shield that returns taught the industry a different death. In the first Halo it is still separate from health, and that precision will later often melt into a single regeneration; teach the 2001 version, not the memory. The game stabilizes the modern console FPS. The stabilization is not the end of the genre's history; it is the moment from which deviations have to apologize.
