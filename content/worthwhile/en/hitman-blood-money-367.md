Agent 47 solves open assassinations using disguises and staged accidents. Levels function as a "clockwork sandbox" in which one can watch routines and create an elegant plan of one's own.

In Hitman: Blood Money every contract is a clock that can be watched. Agent 47 in a disguise stands at the opera, at a carnival, or at a garden party and waits until the routine itself offers an accident. A chandelier, an explosive in a cake, a pistol in silence: an accident is a better score than a firefight, because the newspaper after the mission will write what you left behind.

![Artwork](/img/worthwhile-games/media/367-artwork-1.jpg)

IO Interactive of Copenhagen published it in 2006 through Eidos. Jesper Kyd wrote the music, and the soundtrack in particular holds a gravity that the disguises alone would slide into farce. The consequence of the clockwork is that the plan is the player's, not an arrow in the level. Whoever spends the first hour only watching plays more than the one who immediately draws the silverballers.

In 2006, beside sandboxes and immersive sims, it is the assassination version of the same appetite. A level is a system, not a corridor with one correct shot. Some disguises are binary, and a guard either believes or shoots. The mission structure has not aged, and the 2016 reboot returns to it after Absolution had linearized the series.

![Cover](/img/worthwhile-games/media/367-cover-2.jpg)

Hiding bodies in containers is an economy of space. A corpse on the floor is a trace, and therefore cleanup belongs to the craft as much as the shot does. For level students the opera or a suburban street is a class about loops. When an NPC has no loop, an accident cannot be planned and the game falls into a shooter.

The newspaper after a mission is feedback that is not a number in the corner. You read how the world understood your elegance, or your massacre. The series before and after wavers between an open mission and an action corridor. Blood Money is still the reference point of that first branch.

The large maps have more quiet solutions than loud ones. The game thereby teaches patience as a statistic, even though you never see it on a bar. When you play it today, save before the first shot and then solve the same hour another way. The second solution is the reason the level exists.

Hitman: Blood Money is a 2006 stealth video game developed by IO Interactive and published by Eidos Interactive. It is the fourth installment in the Hitman video game series, and the sequel to 2004's Hitman: Contracts.

Blood Money received critical acclaim for its graphics, narrative, gameplay, voice acting and darker tone; it was also a commercial success, selling more than 2.1 million copies. High-definition ports of Blood Money and two of its predecessors, Hitman 2: Silent Assassin and Contracts, were released on PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360 in January 2013 as the Hitman HD Trilogy.

A sequel, Hitman: Absolution, was released in 2012, and 4K remastered ports of both Blood Money and Absolution were released by Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment in 2019 on the PlayStation 4 and Xbox One as part of the Hitman HD Enhanced Collection.

Blood Money penalizes players for making too much noise or being too violent. New features introduced in Blood Money included the ability to climb over obstacles, improved unarmed combat, the use of non-player characters (NPC) as human shields, the ability to dispose of or conceal bodies, improved character animations, a new game engine, and the ability to upgrade weapons and equipment.

Blood Money provides players with methods to reduce Agent 47's notoriety, including destroying surveillance equipment and bribery. The amount of money the player receives depends on how cleanly the assassination is carried out, with silent killings with no witnesses receiving the highest payout.

Blood Money improved the melee combat system from previous releases, allowing players to disarm as well as lethally throw weapons at NPCs. However, Cayne reveals that the interview was merely a ruse, and that Rick was invited to be given the details concerning a far greater story – the full details on cloned hitman Agent 47, a contract killer for the International Contract Agency (ICA), a global organisation involved in assassinations.

Hitman: Blood Money was announced in November 2004, with a scheduled release for Spring 2005. In March 2006, it was revealed that the game would be released on the Xbox 360, making it the first Hitman game to be released on the seventh generation of video game consoles.

Blood Money was released on the PlayStation 2, Xbox, Xbox 360 and Microsoft Windows on 26 May 2006 in Europe, 30 May in North America, and 1 June in Australia. On 29 January 2013, Blood Money was included in the Hitman HD Trilogy, a compilation also including Hitman 2: Silent Assassin and Hitman: Contracts, released for the PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360.

Blood Money was also included in another compilation, the Hitman HD Enhanced Collection, with Hitman: Absolution, which was released on the PlayStation 4 and Xbox One in January 2019. A port titled Hitman: Blood Money — Reprisal was released for iOS and Android on 30 November 2023 and for Nintendo Switch on 25 January 2024.

The new port introduced revised controls and new features found in later Hitman titles, such as the mini-map and Instinct Mode. Even though "it's not as thematic as the first of the Hitman games, there is enough material here that will satisfy most soundtrack collectors".

Hitman: Blood Money received "generally positive" reviews across all platforms, according to review aggregator platform Metacritic. GameSpot reported that diverse imaginative scenarios gave Blood Money its share of violent thrills.

Hitman: Blood Money sold more than 1.5 million copies by July 17, 2006. Several advertisements promoting Blood Money generated controversy for their violent imagery.

In August 2008, the government of Thailand tagged Hitman a violent video game, along with numerous others, including Resident Evil 4 and, most especially, GTA IV, which had obsessed an 18-year-old man who murdered a taxi driver in Bangkok that month.
