Master Chief and the Arbiter follow the Covenant war from opposing perspectives. Matchmaking and the Xbox Live party system became the model of console online multiplayer.

Halo 2 alternates Master Chief and the Arbiter, an elite warrior of the Covenant. The campaign thereby stops being only a human resistance and becomes a war told from both sides of the visor. On Xbox Live you do not look for a server in a list. Matchmaking places you in a playlist and a party of friends goes with you. That is the social architecture of the console for years ahead.

![Cover](/img/worthwhile-games/media/336-cover-1.jpg)

Bungie published it through Microsoft in 2004. Joseph Staten led the writing and Marty O'Donnell with Michael Salvatori wrote the music again. The Blam engine received a new layer so it could carry larger battles and the shine of armor. The consequence of matchmaking is that networked play stops being the hobby of a server administrator. It becomes a service the console arranges, and other genres take over that model.

Dual wielding and new weapons thicken the arena. Maps such as Lockout stay readable precisely because silhouette and distance still decide more than the number of icons. The campaign ends at the moment when the story is only opening the next war. The cliff at the end is an industrial wound and an authorial decision the players of that year did not forgive at once.

![In the game](/img/worthwhile-games/media/336-screenshot-2.jpg)

Some campaign interiors repeat and have aged. The network model of the party and the playlist has not aged as a norm. In 2004 it is a cultural event of the Xbox, not merely a shooter sequel. Live without this game would be a promise. With it, it is a habit.

The Arbiter forces you to play as a body the previous game taught you to kill. Empathy here is mechanical: you have his armor and his enemies, not only a cutscene about his sadness. For multiplayer students matchmaking is a required class. A server list and an automatic queue are two different social contracts.

The balance of the battle rifle and the pistols was tuned as a metagame. The campaign is a film, the network is a sport, and Bungie that year holds both in one box. When you play it today, start a network map first and only then the campaign. The historical center of gravity is in how the game seats you with strangers without a single click on an IP address.
