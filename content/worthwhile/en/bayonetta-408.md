The witch Bayonetta fights angels with extravagant combos. Dodge-offset and Witch Time reward risky aggression and the preservation of a combo across dodges.

Bayonetta, a witch in glasses with pistols on her heels, cuts down angels in long combos. The player presses attacks in rhythm, dodges at the last moment, and continues the combination straight out of that dodge. Witch Time slows the world after a close evade. The reward for risk is not only survival, but a window in which the combo continues and the damage grows.

PlatinumGames was led by Hideki Kamiya, a director with a past at Capcom and at the closed studio Clover. With Atsushi Inaba and other Clover people they founded Platinum, and Sega published the game in 2009. The combat system is the handwork of animators and cancel windows, not a physics simulation. In 2009 on PS3 and Xbox 360 it is an answer to action games that lock the player into short cinematic strikes.

Dodge offset is a concrete decision: a dodge does not break the combo. The consequence is aggressive play that is not rewarded for passively waiting to counter, but for an attack that has an escape inside it. The heel guns and the hair, which is both clothing and demon, turn every long animation into a joke about how far spectacle can go without breaking the readability of hits.

The Japanese release in October 2009 arrives in a year when console action often means cover or an open world. This is a short, dense, almost arcade campaign. The tone is deliberately overshot. The studio bets that the player will recognize the craft in the combos, even when the angels and the one-note jokes tire them for stretches.

Some cutscenes have aged, as has the repetition of combat rooms in the later campaign. The camera in tighter spaces also sometimes fails to keep up with its own spectacle. Witch Time and the offset hold. Later character-action games still explain their dodges in this vocabulary.

Difficulty and chapter rankings make you repeat a fight not for loot, but for cleanliness of execution. That is an old arcade promise inside a modern console game. In class, set it beside hack-and-slash games that punish a mistake with a restart. Here, a mistake you narrowly avoid is part of the optimal sentence.

It is developed by PlatinumGames, owned by Sega, and, since the release of Bayonetta 2 in 2014, published by Nintendo. The franchise was introduced in 2009 with Bayonetta, which was followed by two sequels, Bayonetta 2 (2014) and Bayonetta 3 (2022), as well as a prequel, Bayonetta Origins: Cereza and the Lost Demon (2023).

Bayonetta 2 was announced in 2012 during a Nintendo Direct for the Wii U. A prequel companion game to Bayonetta 3, focusing on Cereza as a young girl, was announced by Nintendo at The Game Awards 2022.

An action/shooter 16-bit-style browser minigame called "8-Bit Bayonetta" was released on February 16, 2015, and later on Steam on March 31, 2017. Outside of the Bayonetta series, Bayonetta characters have made guest appearances in other Sega and PlatinumGames releases.

Bayonetta appears as a downloadable character in the multiplayer fighting game Anarchy Reigns. Both games also feature a stage based on the falling Umbra Clock Tower which appeared in the first two Bayonetta games.

Bayonetta will be added to Sonic Racing CrossWorlds as a playable character via a forthcoming free update. An anime film based on the first game, titled Bayonetta: Bloody Fate, was produced by Gonzo and released in 2013 in workings with Sega.

In 2014, Udon Entertainment released a book titled The Eyes of Bayonetta, which included a list of her abilities. Bayonetta, Bayonetta 2, and Bayonetta 3 each received generally positive reviews from critics according to review aggregator Metacritic.

Bayonetta 2 was nominated for Game of the Year at The Game Awards 2014, though it ultimately lost to Dragon Age: Inquisition. The release of Bayonetta 3 was preceded by controversy after Hellena Taylor, who voiced the character in the first two games, publicly accused PlatinumGames of offering her an "insulting" sum to reprise the role and called on fans to boycott the game.
