Nintendo EPD under Hidemaro Fujibayashi and Eiji Aonuma released the game in May 2023 on Switch. It is a sequel that does not only recycle the map, but a question: what if the chemistry of the previous game is not enough and the player should receive a workshop. Physical construction is a technological risk on hardware that in 2023 is no longer new. Large machines fall in frame rate and are still the center of the game, because the studio bets on invention, not on a stable sixty frames.

The decision to let the player glue a rocket to a shield means the designer cannot predict every win. The consequence is a freedom that teaches engineering, and also shrines an experienced player flies over in a way that bypasses the intended lesson. Zonai devices, fans, wheels, and batteries, are the vocabulary of the building set. Without energy an idea is only a statue, which keeps an economy even in an otherwise generous world.

In 2023 the game answers Breath of the Wild not with more icons, but with a new verb of manufacture.
