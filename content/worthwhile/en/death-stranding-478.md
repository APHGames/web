Sam Porter Bridges connects an isolated America by transporting deliveries. Terrain, cargo, and balance make walking itself a system; the asynchronous structures of other players turn loneliness into cooperation.

Sam Porter Bridges in Death Stranding carries packages across rocks, rivers, and slopes, and the player holds balance so the cargo does not tip. Walking is a system: a ladder, a rope, a generator, and later a road you built because you got tired of carrying it on your back. The asynchronous world shows other players' structures, bridges, and stairs without you meeting them. Loneliness is the base map, and someone else's help is a trace, not a teammate in voice chat.

![In the game](/img/worthwhile-games/media/478-screenshot-1.jpg)

Kojima Productions and Hideo Kojima released the game through Sony in November 2019 on PlayStation 4. It runs on the Decima engine, which Guerrilla used for Horizon, and Norman Reedus plays Sam. After the split with Konami, the studio builds the first big game under its own name. The decision to make terrain and cargo the main obstacle means the weapon and the story are the surroundings. The consequence is a game some people turn off after an hour and some take as the most important change of verb in the decade: to arrive.

BT zones and cargo that rattles when you run join horror with logistics. The quiet path and the loud path are two different risks, both about weight. The chiral network gradually turns civilization into a multiplayer you did not see. A like on someone else's bridge is the entire social contract.

![Cover](/img/worthwhile-games/media/478-cover-2.jpg)

In 2019 this is the most anticipated oddity from the author of Metal Gear. Beside services that want a daily reward, it offers a long walk and celebrities in the service of a very personal theme of connection. Later vehicles and weapons soften the opening punishment. Anyone who lasts through the first region on foot receives tools that turn the expedition into the administration of a territory.

The density of exposition has aged, as has some of the casting, which draws attention away from the slope. Some contracts are also a repetition of the same delivery once the road is already standing. Weight and shared infrastructure hold. It is still the cleanest big example of how to turn transport into cooperation between people who will not meet.

America as a broken continent is both the theme and the layout of the missions. The studio does not need the open life of a city; it needs the distance between two shelters. In class, use it for a verb games usually skip. Ask what happens to difficulty when you subtract the combat and leave only the slope and the pack.
