Geralt in The Witcher 3 searches for Ciri across Velen, Novigrad, and Skellige, and most of the time the player is not on the main line but in a side quest that has its own injury, joke, and consequence. Combat with signs, oils, and a sword is an action-RPG layer on top of a game whose real currency is decisions in dialogue and whom you return to later.

CD Projekt Red under director Konrad Tomaszkiewicz released the game in May 2015 on REDengine 3. The Polish studio continues Andrzej Sapkowski's books and its own previous games, but for the first time it opens a truly large world and publishes it itself. Before release there was a public argument that the game looked less impressive than older demonstrations. The engine itself still carries three large regions, weather, and city crowds that in 2015 set the expectation for an open RPG.

The decision to write side quests as short dramas, not as loot collection on a map, means the content is expensive and slow. The consequence is a world in which the Bloody Baron stays in memory longer than many main villains of other games. Gwent is a game inside the game and also a way to turn inns into a network. It is not required, but anyone who accepts it reads the economy of the continent differently.

In 2015 the game raises the bar for what a player expects from a side quest in a big RPG. Hearts of Stone the same year and Blood and Wine in 2016 extend that promise instead of letting an expansion empty it. Roach, the inventory, and movement in cramped villages show that the open world still fights the controls. The studio bets on writing where the horse's animation is not enough.

The riding has aged, as have parts of the combat and the inventory interface. Some question marks on the map are also more ordinary than the best contracts. The quality of individual stories holds, and so does the willingness to let a choice mature a region later. That is why the game still returns as a measure, not only as a hit of one May.

The Polish studio is part of the history of the genre: the big Western market here receives an RPG that was not made in California or Japan and that speaks its own cynicism. In class, pick one side quest and one question mark on the map. The difference between them is a whole lesson in what an open world promises and what it actually writes.

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt is a 2015 action role-playing game developed and published by CD Projekt. It is the sequel to the 2011 game The Witcher 2: Assassins of Kings and the third game in The Witcher video game series, played in an open world with a third-person perspective.

The games follow the Witcher series of fantasy novels by Polish author Andrzej Sapkowski. Players control Geralt of Rivia, a monster slayer for hire known as a Witcher, and search for his adopted daughter, Ciri, who is on the run from the Wild Hunt.

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt was released for PlayStation 4, Windows, and Xbox One in May 2015, with a Nintendo Switch version released in October 2019, and PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S versions (subtitled "Complete Edition") released in December 2022. A third expansion, Songs of the Past, is scheduled for a 2027 release, and a sequel, The Witcher IV, is in development.
