Emily or Corvo retake the throne in Karnaca. The Clockwork Mansion and A Crack in the Slab are exemplary levels in which the architecture itself becomes a mechanical system.

In Dishonored 2 you play Emily or Corvo and in Karnaca you again assemble your own path through a building. The player shortens roofs with Blink or Far Reach, and in two levels runs into architecture that is itself a machine. Jindosh's clockwork mansion rearranges according to which lever you turn. A Crack in the Slab lets you move between two times of the same house, so an object placed now is a trap or a key a decade earlier.

![Cover](/img/worthwhile-games/media/457-cover-1.jpg)

Arkane Lyon under Harvey Smith released the game through Bethesda in November 2016. It continues from Dunwall, but the city, the dust, and the bone charms are different; the contract of multiple solutions remains. The Void Engine, built on id Tech, is supposed to carry dense systemic levels. The PC release is nevertheless accompanied by performance problems that become part of the first reputation as much as the praise of two missions.

The decision to turn the mansion and the time house into mechanical toys means level design is both a puzzle and a weapon. The consequence is that the rest of the campaign is measured against them, which is flattering and dangerous for the studio. Emily and Corvo do not have the same abilities. A second playthrough is not only a different morality; it is a different vocabulary of distance and trickery.

In 2016 the immersive simulation is still a minority big genre. The second game proves that a sequel can be mainly about two unforgettable buildings, not about a bigger map. The nonviolent path is fully valid again. The studio still refuses to punish silence as a less valuable game, even though chaos changes the tone of the ending.

That technical start on PC has aged, as have some streets between the big missions, which no longer surprise as much. Awareness of the series also stays narrower than that of the open worlds of the same year. The Clockwork Mansion and the crack in time hold. They are still the levels you give a student when they need to understand that architecture can have a state.

Bone charms and their nesting add a layer of character building that argues with how you want to pass a specific room. Equipment is a sentence about style, not only a number. In class, play only those two levels if there is no time for the campaign. The rest of the game is good context; those two are the argument.
