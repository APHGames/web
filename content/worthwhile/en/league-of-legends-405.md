Two five-member teams of heroes destroy the opponent's base. A free-to-play live-service model, regular updates, and esports fundamentally transformed the structure of the PC multiplayer market.

Two teams of five heroes destroy towers, last-hit enemies, and finally the base. The player holds a lane, buys items with gold from last hits, and watches when to leave the map before getting picked off. One match lasts tens of minutes and teaches the same map over and over. Knowledge of Summoner's Rift is part of your power, not cosmetics.

Riot Games, led by Brandon Beck and Marc Merrill, built the game on the legacy of the Defense of the Ancients mod for Warcraft III. Steve Feak, who had carried that mod in the community, took part in the design. The custom client in 2009 is not a technical marvel. What matters more is that the game runs as a service: an account, a rotation of heroes, regular patches, and gradually a large competitive scene.

The decision to ship free and earn money on heroes and cosmetics opened the genre to people who would not have paid for a boxed strategy title. The consequence is an enormous player base, and also an economy that becomes part of the meta. The heroes are extremely asymmetrical, but the map and the objectives stay stable. You therefore learn new characters against old geometry, which is why it can be played for years.

In the autumn of 2009 League of Legends is still a bet beside the paid model. Within a few years it becomes the main shape of PC multiplayer and a template for the live-service games that follow it. Esports is not a tournament glued on at the end of the year. The ladder, the draft, and the spectator readability of abilities are part of the product from the beginning of its adolescence.

The original client has aged, as has some of the early presentation and the idea that toxic chat is merely a companion to competition. The concrete metas of individual seasons are also period documents. The loop of lane, objective, and team fight holds. It is still the cleanest example of how to turn a mod inside someone else's strategy game into an industry of its own.

Regularly adding heroes changes the game without throwing away the map. That is a different relationship to a sequel than a one-off second game in a box. In class, take it as an economic event as well as a design event: free-to-play here is not a discount, but the way the MOBA genre became public infrastructure.

Since its release in October 2009, League has been free-to-play and is monetized through purchasable character customization. League of Legends has received generally favorable reviews, which have highlighted its accessibility, character designs, and production value.

In 2019, League regularly peaked at eight million concurrent players, and its popularity has led to tie-ins such as music, comic books, short stories, and the animated series Arcane. League of Legends is the world's largest esport, with an international competitive scene consisting of multiple regional leagues which culminates in an annual League of Legends World Championship.

League of Legends is a multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) game in which the player controls a character ("champion") with a set of unique abilities from an isometric perspective. Summoner's Rift is the flagship game mode of League of Legends and the most prominent in professional-level play.

Besides Summoner's Rift, League of Legends has two other permanent game modes. League of Legends Classic, a mode restoring the visuals and gameplay of the game's early life, was announced in June 2026, with the full release set for late July.

A demonstration of League of Legends built in the Warcraft III game engine was completed in four months and then shown at the 2007 Game Developers Conference. The game's full name was announced as League of Legends: Clash of Fates.

The same day, the League of Legends team published a video responding to the report, and saying they intended to replace the game's client, improve onboarding for new users, and overhaul visuals. Riot Games added an additional skin tier to League of Legends in 2024, describing them as a luxury good.

League of Legends received generally favorable reviews on its initial release, according to review aggregator website Metacritic. Comparing it to Defense of the Ancients, Rick McCormick of GamesRadar+ said that playing League of Legends was "a vote for choice over refinement".

According to GamesRadar+ and GameSpot, League of Legends would feel familiar to those who had already played DotA. IGN Steve Butts compared the launch to the poor state of CrimeCraft release earlier in 2009; he indicated that features available during League of Legends beta were removed for the release, even for those who purchased the retail version.

GameSpot Tyler Hicks wrote that new players would pick up League of Legends quicker than DotA and that the removal of randomness-based skills made the game more competitive. League of Legends has received awards for its contribution to esports.

Also at The Game Awards, the Riot won Best Esports Event for the 2019, 2020 and 2021 League World Championships. League of Legends s player base has a longstanding reputation for "toxicity"—rude and abusive in-game behavior, with a survey by the Anti-Defamation League indicating that 76% of players have experienced in-game harassment.

For the 2019 and 2020 League of Legends World Championship finals, Riot Games reported 44 and 45 peak million concurrent viewers respectively. Harvard Business Review said that League of Legends epitomized the birth of the esports industry.

League games are typically livestreamed on platforms such as Twitch and YouTube. In 2020, his team's slot in the North American league was sold to the Evil Geniuses organization for $33 million.

For the 10th anniversary of League of Legends in 2019, Riot Games announced several games at various stages of production that were directly related to the League of Legends intellectual property (IP). League of Legends: Wild Rift is a version of the game for mobile operating systems Android and iOS.

Instead of porting the game from League of Legends, Wild Rift s character models and environments were entirely rebuilt. It was the first title released under Riot Games's publishing arm, Riot Forge, wherein non-Riot studios develop games using League of Legends characters.

Song of Nunu: A League of Legends Story, a third-person adventure game revolving around the champion Nunu's search for his mother, with the help of the yeti Willump, was announced for a planned release in 2022. The group's debut single, "Pop/Stars", which premiered at the 2018 League of Legends World Championship, garnered over 400 million views on YouTube and sparked widespread interest from people unfamiliar with League of Legends.

The vocalists—Keke Palmer, Thutmose, Becky G, Duckwrth, and Soyeon—performed a live version of the group's song, "Giants", during the opening ceremony of the 2019 League of Legends World Championship, alongside holographic versions of their characters. While celebrating League of Legends tenth anniversary, Riot announced an animated television series, Arcane.
