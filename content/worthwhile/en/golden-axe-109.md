Sega sent the game to arcades in 1989. Makoto Uchida led the design, and the cabinet assumes two players will stand at it. The hardware draws large sprites and short levels. There is no world map; there is a street, a bridge, and a fortress, walked through on coins.

The decision to carry the beat 'em up into fantasy has a consequence for the genre. A sword and a lizard are not just a costume on a street brawl; they change the reach and the rhythm of a strike.
