The Kid restores a world broken by the Calamity. A narrator reacting almost in real time to the player's actions makes commentary part of dynamic feedback.

In Bastion the Kid wakes after the Calamity and gathers the shards of the world, while a narrator comments on almost every step, every fall off the edge, and every choice of weapon. From an isometric view the player strikes, shoots, and at the end of a level chooses whether to look at a memory or strengthen a weapon. The world assembles under your feet only when you step onto it.

![Cover](/img/worthwhile-games/media/423-cover-1.jpg)

Supergiant Games, the small studio of Amir Rao and Gavin Simon with a past at EA, released its first game in July 2011 on Xbox Live Arcade through Warner Bros. Greg Kasavin wrote the script, Logan Cunningham plays the narrator, and Darren Korb wrote the music. The isometric action is not a technical breakthrough. The breakthrough is the audio layer, written as a reaction to game states, so the commentary feels as if someone in the room were watching.

The decision to make the narrator an interface means the tutorial does not have to be a window with text. The consequence is that even a repeated strike gets a sentence, and the world feels inhabited even though you are almost alone in it. Idols on another playthrough change the rules and raise the difficulty on purpose. The studio thereby offers new game plus as design, not as a cosmetic damage number.

In 2011 this is proof that a downloadable console game at a fraction of a blockbuster budget can have a stronger voice than an open world. Shortness is part of the form. Caelondia and its factions are told in fragments. The player never receives an encyclopedia; they receive a tone, a song, and a choice that hurts more than a fight.

The isometric combat itself has aged, and beside the studio's later games it is simple. The repetition of arenas also reveals a small team after a while. The narrator, the music, and the structure of a short action RPG that knows when to end all hold. That is a lesson big studios often cannot keep.

The weapons differ substantially in rhythm, not only in a number. The hammer and the bow are two different games on the same map, and the narrator notes the change. In class, play it for one question: what can a voice do when it is tied to events and not to a scene recorded in advance.

Bastion is a 2011 action role-playing video game developed by independent developer Supergiant Games and originally published by Warner Bros. Bastion s story follows the Kid as he collects special shards of rock to power a structure, the Bastion, in the wake of an apocalyptic Calamity.

Bastion was published in July 2011 for Xbox 360 via Xbox Live Arcade, and in August 2011 through digital distribution for Microsoft Windows on Steam. Bastion s soundtrack was produced and composed by Darren Korb, and a soundtrack album was made available for sale in August 2011.

Bastion has won many nominations and awards since its release, including several for best downloadable game and best music, from review outlets such as IGN and Game Informer as well as from the Spike Video Game Awards, the Game Developers Conference, and the Academy of Interactive Arts & Sciences.
