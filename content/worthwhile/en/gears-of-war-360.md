Marcus Fenix fights the Locust with cover and heavy weapons. "Sticky cover," the roadie run, and the pronounced weight of the animations standardized the cover TPS for an entire generation.

In Gears of War, Marcus Fenix sticks to a wall and fires from behind it, blind or after a lean. Cover is not advice; it is a state the game snaps you into. The roadie run and active reload, a small timing game on the reload key, give the heavy body two exceptions: a short sprint and a reward for attention while reloading.

![In the game](/img/worthwhile-games/media/360-screenshot-1.jpg)

Epic Games published it in 2006 through Microsoft on the Xbox 360. Cliff Bleszinski led the design in public, and the game is a display case for Unreal Engine 3, which is supposed to show that generation what the new hardware can carry. The consequence of sticky cover is that a level is built as a sequence of arenas with walls at the right height. The space that in Resident Evil 4 belonged to your feet belongs here to a button.

In 2006, a year after the Xbox 360 launched, this is the definition of adult action for that generation: shoulders in the frame, a gray-brown palette, a chainsaw gun. The industry will copy that image until it becomes a cliché. The palette and some of the cover corridors have aged precisely because everyone copied them. Active reload and the weight of the animation still have a reason, and that cannot be copied so easily.

![Cover](/img/worthwhile-games/media/360-cover-2.jpg)

The Locust and Emergence are a pretext for bodies that climb out of walls. The horror here is bodily, not psychological, and therefore it fits cover: you are afraid of the open ground. For level students the chest-high wall is a textbook and a warning. Once every room is the same little wall, the system that in 2006 felt like a new verb starts being furniture.

Multiplayer is as important as the campaign. Cover on the network is a sport about who leaves the wall first, and the campaign is the tutorial of that sport. The series then adds Horde and further modes. The first game is still the one that in a single year standardized sticky cover for a whole generation of third-person games.

The art bets on the silhouette of the shoulder and on the chainsaw. Readability in chaos is the reason the character is so wide, not only the taste of the period. When you play it today, notice when the game lets you off cover by itself. In those seconds freedom ends and the direction Epic sold to a whole generation begins.

Gears of War (also referred to as Gears) is a media franchise centered on a series of video games created by Epic Games, developed and managed by The Coalition, and owned and published by Xbox Game Studios. The first installment, Gears of War, was released on November 7, 2006, for the Xbox 360.

Two subsequent titles, Gears of War 2 (2008) and Gears of War 3 (2011), featured a three-way conflict between humanity, the Locust Horde and their mutated counterparts, the Lambent. Gears of War: Judgment, a spin-off prequel to the series' first title, was released in 2013; it focuses on Damon Baird, one of Fenix's squad-mates.

Gears of War: Ultimate Edition was released for the Xbox One and Microsoft Windows between August 2015 to March 2016. The fourth installment in the main series, Gears of War 4, is set 25 years after Gears of War 3 and follows Marcus Fenix's son, JD and his friends as they battle security forces deployed by a totalitarian COG government as well as the Swarm, a reconstituted version of the Locust Horde that once again threatens humanity.

Gears 5 (2019) is the direct sequel to Gears of War 4 and revolves around Kait Diaz, a friend of JD, who embarks on an adventure to learn the truth about her past and the connections between her history and the Locust Horde. The first four installments of the Gears of War series used a modified version of the Unreal Engine 3 engine.

Canadian studio The Coalition developed Gears of War 4, which was released on October 11, 2016, for the Xbox One and Windows 10. All six installments in Gears of War featured several multiplayer modes that allowed players to compete against each other or team-up to battle AI opponents on Xbox Live.

Gears of War became one of the best-selling franchises for the Xbox 360. The Gears of War games have been amongst the most popular and most played titles on Xbox Live.

The Gears of War series takes place on Sera, a fictional Earth-like planet. Gears of War is set 14 years after the Locust emerged, and four years into Marcus Fenix's imprisonment.

Gears of War: RAAM's Shadow is set nine years after E-Day, shortly before the Locust invade Ephyra. Gears of War: Judgment focuses on Baird and Cole during the early months of the Locust War.

A separate campaign, Aftermath, features Baird, Cole, and Paduk in the hours before the activation of the Imulsion countermeasure in Gears of War 3 as they prepare for reinforcements in the upcoming battle of Azura. Gears of War: E-Day is a prequel which takes place fourteen years before the first game on Emergence Day, the day on which the Locust Horde emerged on the surface of Sera to wage war against humanity, and follows Marcus Fenix and Dominic Santiago as young men.

Gears 5 reveals that JD, Del, and Marcus rejoin the COG, along with Kait as an Outsider recruit, in order to help fight the Swarm. Gears 5: Hivebusters follows Jeremiah Keegan, Lahni Kaliso, and Leslie Macallister shortly after the Battle of New Ephyra.

Players in Gears of War can carry four different weapons, with the exception of the fourth game, allowing two primary weapon slots that can carry weapons which include, but are not limited to: assault rifles, shotguns, a sniper rifle, grenade launchers and an explosive bow; one grenade slot, which may be filled with up to four grenades of a specific type (Fragmentation, Smoke, Ink and Incendiary), and one pistol-type weapon.

While most of the weapons are based on standard shooter archetypes, Gears of War s signature weapon is the Lancer Assault Rifle, which has a mounted chainsaw bayonet that can be used in close quarter combat to instantly kill a standard foe. In Gears of War 2 and Gears of War 3, all four types of grenades can be planted on any reachable surface, detonating when an opponent comes close to it.

All Gears of War games feature a campaign mode that can be played cooperatively with one other player. The third and fourth players in Gears of War 3 will be separated between the paths of the first and second players.

The competitive multiplayer mode in Gears of War features 8 players while in Gears of War 2 features up to ten players split between COG and Locust forces in a number of gameplay types. In the mode "Guardian" (only in Gears of War 2) one member of each team is designated as the leader.
